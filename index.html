Questions

What people ask before they travel.

Will I know the price before I travel?

Yes. After your photo assessment you receive a written plan with your treatment, appointments, days in Cancún and price. It also says clearly what is not included, such as flights, hotel and meals, so there are no surprises.

Do I need a passport?

Yes. Flying from the United States or Canada to Mexico requires a valid passport. Check your country's requirements before buying your flight.

Does the team speak English?

Yes. We look after you in English and Spanish throughout the process, from your first message to your follow-up at home.

Can someone travel with me?

Yes. Your companion is welcome at the clinic and during transfers.

What if I need an adjustment once I'm home?

We stay in touch after your treatment. If a question or adjustment comes up, we review it with you by video and agree on next steps, according to your treatment and its warranty.

Who places the implants?

Treatment is led by Dr. Carolina Aguirre, with Dr. Edgar Mendoza in implantology. We use Neodent and Alpha-Bio Tec implant systems and plan with 3D imaging.
